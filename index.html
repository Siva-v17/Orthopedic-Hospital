Know the signs

When should you see an orthopedic doctor?

Do not ignore these warning signs. Early diagnosis often means simpler treatment.

🦵

Knee pain on stairs

Stiffness or swelling lasting more than two weeks.

🔙

Persistent back pain

Pain spreading to the leg, or numbness and tingling.

🤕

Injury that won't settle

Swelling, bruising or inability to bear weight after a fall.

🖐️

Shoulder or wrist pain

Reduced movement, clicking or night-time ache.

🧒

Child's gait or posture

Limping, flat feet or uneven shoulders and hips.

🦴

Fragile bones

Frequent fractures or height loss after 50 years of age.

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly with any orthopedic specialist online or by phone.

How soon can I walk after knee replacement?

Most patients stand and walk with support within 24 hours under our enhanced-recovery protocol.

Is emergency trauma care available at night?

Yes. Our trauma unit with on-call surgeons and imaging runs 24x7, every day of the year.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Tele-orthopedic consults are available for follow-ups and second opinions.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Previous scans, reports, a list of current medicines and your insurance card if applicable.
