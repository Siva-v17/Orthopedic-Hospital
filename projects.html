  • Pre-operative 3D planning for every case

FAQ

About our programs

Are the rural camps really free?

Yes. Screening, consultation and basic medicines at our camps are free for all attendees.

How can my village host a camp?

Contact our outreach team through the Contact page and we will plan dates, venue and logistics with you.

Can outside surgeons join the training academy?

Yes. Fellowships and short courses are open to qualified orthopedic surgeons. Apply through the Contact page.

Can patients take part in research trials?

Participation is always voluntary and requires informed consent under an approved clinical protocol.

Have a program idea or partnership in mind?

Tell us how you would like to collaborate and our team will get back to you.
