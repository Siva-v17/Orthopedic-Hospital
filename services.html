FAQ

Service questions answered

Is surgery always required?

No. Most patients improve with medication, injections and physiotherapy; surgery is advised only when necessary.

How long is recovery after joint replacement?

Most patients return to daily activity in 4 to 6 weeks, with full recovery in about 3 months.

Do you offer physiotherapy at home?

Yes. Our therapists provide home visits and video-guided programs after discharge.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Share your scans through a tele-consult and a senior surgeon will review your case.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. Our insurance desk manages cashless approvals and paperwork with major insurers and government schemes.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the Contact page or call us. Our care coordinators will confirm a slot with the right specialist.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring previous reports, scans, a list of current medicines and any insurance documents.

Are health packages available for families?

Yes. Our packages can be arranged for parents and family members. Ask the care desk for combined slots.

Not sure which service you need?

Our care coordinators will match you with the right specialist.
